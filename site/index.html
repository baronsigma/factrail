Trade · early version

Assess an EU import

Structure a pre-import decision: classification, duty, VAT, compliance, landed-cost and risk considerations. Any origin; EU destinations, with France best supported. Missing inputs and unavailable checks are stated explicitly.

assess_import

Import assessments are indicative, not binding customs decisions. Some live trade source checks are still unavailable. Always verify rates, classification and obligations against official sources before acting.
