Why FACTRAIL

Evidence an agent can inspect and reuse.

Agents should not have to choose between blindly trusting model output and rebuilding source verification from scratch. FACTRAIL provides a stable evidence layer between agents and real-world data sources. It returns what was established, where it came from, how current it is, what remains unresolved, and a receipt another agent can inspect later.

Trust model

Unknown is better than invented.

Sources stay visibleSource identity, support level, freshness, and conflicts travel with the result.
Quality propagatesCaller input is not external verification. Derived results inherit the quality of their inputs.
Failures are explicitMissing input, unsupported scope, stale data, and source failures have distinct outcomes.

A deterministic calculation is not automatically authoritative. Secondary sources are not silently promoted. Receipts let you check content integrity; they are not signatures or claims of absolute truth.

Sources and coverage

Source authority is part of the result.

French company verification uses INSEE / Sirene and BODACC, with field-specific authority handling. Trade/import assessment is beta. FACTRAIL includes validated official TARIC snapshot ingestion infrastructure, but no genuine European Commission snapshot is currently installed. Access2Markets is secondary evidence; curated tariff and VAT references remain curated or provisional. No authoritative EU VAT integration or complete authoritative landed-cost chain is available.

Evidence Contract

Reusable, integrity-checkable receipts.

EvidenceEnvelope reports status, subject, facts, sources, conflicts, coverage, freshness, and a receipt ID. Receipt IDs are content-addressed; retrieval recomputes the canonical hash and rejects altered content. This protects content integrity, not the truth of a claim or a source's correctness.
